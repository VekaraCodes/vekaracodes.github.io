Privacy Policy & Data Security Statement

LexiconTriTrainer for Android

Publisher: VekaraCodes  ·  Effective date: 18 October 2026

1. Overview and scope

This Privacy Policy describes how LexiconTriTrainer (the “App”), published by VekaraCodes (the “Developer”), accesses, uses, stores, shares and deletes information during its operation. It applies to the Android application, including testing releases.

Privacy at a glance

LexiconTriTrainer is designed for offline vocabulary practice. Vocabulary lists, learning progress and preferences are stored on the device. The App does not require registration, and its core functions do not use a developer-operated online account or synchronization service.

2. Information handled by the App

InformationWhy it is usedWhere it is stored
Vocabulary words, definitions, translations and imported text filesTo provide learning exercises and manage user-selected vocabulary listsApp-private local file storage
Learning progress and repetition schedulesTo track practice and determine when vocabulary should be reviewedAndroid SharedPreferences on the device
App preferences and exercise settingsTo remember selected modes and application behaviorAndroid SharedPreferences on the device

The content of vocabulary files is determined by the user or supplied as example learning material. Users should avoid importing sensitive personal information they do not wish to keep on their device.

3. Collection, transmission and sharing

The App does not require a user account and does not offer developer-hosted cloud synchronization. Its vocabulary and learning-progress functions do not intentionally transmit vocabulary content, learning statistics or saved settings to servers operated by VekaraCodes. The Developer does not sell users’ vocabulary or learning-progress information.

The App provides an import and export feature using Android’s system document picker. An imported file is read from the location selected by the user and copied into the App’s local storage. When a user exports a file, the destination is chosen by the user. If that destination is a third-party or cloud storage provider, the provider may process the exported file under its own privacy terms.

Information may also be handled by Android backup or device-transfer services as described in Section 5. The Developer does not control the privacy practices of these services.

4. Data security

The App uses Android’s application-private storage for its working vocabulary files and saved preferences. Android’s operating-system safeguards restrict ordinary access by other applications. The App does not provide an online user database or a developer-operated remote access service.

Local storage protection depends in part on the device, Android version, system security settings and whether the device is locked or compromised. The App does not claim to provide separate end-to-end encryption of files. Copies exported outside the App are subject to the security and access controls of the user-selected destination.

5. Android backups and device transfers

Depending on the device, Android version, user settings and the App’s final backup configuration, app-private files and preferences may be included in operating-system backups or transferred when setting up another device. If cloud backup is enabled, a provider such as Google may process and retain backup data subject to its own terms and policies.

Android backup and restoration are separate from any Developer-operated data collection or synchronization service. Users can review their device’s backup settings to understand what may be backed up.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored vocabulary files and learning preferences generally remain on the device until they are removed by the user, the application data is cleared, or the App is uninstalled.

  • Delete a vocabulary list: use the App’s file-management interface to delete a saved vocabulary file. Its associated locally stored repetition records are removed when the deletion succeeds.
  • Reset learning progress: use the App’s reset option for the selected vocabulary list.
  • Remove local App data: clear the App’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the App, subject to Android’s backup and restoration behavior.
  • Previously exported files: delete these separately from the destination to which they were exported.

Copies held in device backups or by external storage providers may remain subject to those providers’ retention and deletion practices.
