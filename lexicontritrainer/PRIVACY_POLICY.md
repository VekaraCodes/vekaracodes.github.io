# Privacy Policy — LexiconTriTrainer

**Last updated:** 8 November 2026  
**Developer:** VekaraCodes  
**App:** LexiconTriTrainer (Android)  
**Website:** https://vekaracodes.github.io/lexicontritrainer/

## At a glance

LexiconTriTrainer is designed to work offline, without an account, advertisements, or in-app analytics. Your vocabulary and learning progress are stored locally. Data may also be handled by Android's optional backup system or by the storage location you choose when exporting files.

## 1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the **LexiconTriTrainer** Android application and its project website. The app is developed and published under the name **VekaraCodes**. This document explains what information the app handles on your device, what may leave your device through actions you choose, and what happens when you visit the website.

## 2. Information stored on your device

To make the app work, LexiconTriTrainer processes and stores locally:

- **Vocabulary files:** built-in example lists and text wordlists you choose to import, stored in the app's private file area.
- **Learning progress:** spaced-repetition timestamps and review state associated with vocabulary entries.
- **Preferences:** settings such as selected practice mode, question preferences, and spaced-repetition options.
- **Practice information:** answers and session statistics used by the app while you study.

This information is used to display exercises, keep track of progress, and remember settings. The application does not require a name, email address, or account to study. If you place personal information in a wordlist yourself, the app will process that content locally as part of the vocabulary file.

## 3. Data transfers and sharing

The app's normal learning functions do not require an internet connection. It is not designed to upload vocabulary, answers, or progress to VekaraCodes servers. Its current design does not include advertising, analytics, or account-based synchronization. VekaraCodes does not sell or share your wordlists or progress with advertisers.

**Files you import or export:** Android's document picker lets you choose a file to import. The app copies it into local app storage. When you export a file, Android lets you choose the destination. If you select a cloud-backed provider, it may store a copy subject to its own privacy practices. These actions are initiated by you.

## 4. Backups, retention, and deletion

Imported wordlists and preferences remain on the device while the app data is kept. Learning progress can be reset, and individual imported wordlists can be deleted through the app's controls. Uninstalling the app ordinarily removes its private local storage.

**Android backups are separate:** depending on your Android version, device settings, and the app's backup configuration, Android or your device manufacturer may back up app files and preferences. The operating system and the chosen backup provider control this process. Restoring a backup may restore the app data after reinstalling.

To remove local information, use the app's delete/reset controls or uninstall it. To manage backups, use your device's backup settings and provider tools. Files separately exported need to be removed at their chosen destination as well.

## 5. Visiting this website

The static website is hosted on **GitHub Pages**. It does not require an account, set first-party tracking cookies, load third-party advertising scripts, or run first-party analytics. The website serves its own CSS and uses a small script that reads the wordlist catalogue from the same website.

GitHub may process connection information such as IP addresses, browser details, and page requests for hosting and security. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Following external links brings you to services with their own policies. Downloading a wordlist from the website does not automatically send it from the Android app to the website.

## 6. Contact, questions, and privacy requests

If you voluntarily contact VekaraCodes, the information and message you provide may be used to respond and provide support. Such correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably needed, subject to the relevant platform provider's retention and applicable legal requirements, and is not used for advertising.

Contact VekaraCodes via the contact options on the [developer GitHub profile](https://github.com/VekaraCodes). Use a **private channel** for personal information; public GitHub issues are not appropriate for sensitive information.

Where applicable under data-protection law, you may request access to, correction, or deletion of personal information VekaraCodes actually holds, such as voluntary support messages. VekaraCodes does not hold a cloud copy of in-app vocabulary or progress and cannot erase local content remotely.

## 7. Changes to this policy

This policy may change when the app or website changes. The date above will be updated. If internet features, analytics, accounts, or other data processing are added, this policy and the relevant store disclosures should be updated accordingly.
