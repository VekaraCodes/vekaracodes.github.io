Your information matters

Privacy policy.

How LexiconTriTrainer handles your vocabulary, study progress, and information when you visit this website.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

On this pageAt a glanceInformation on your deviceData transfers and sharingBackups and deletionWebsite and GitHub PagesContact and requestsChanges to this policy

The short version: LexiconTriTrainer is designed to work offline, without an account, advertisements, or in-app analytics. Your vocabulary and learning progress are stored locally. Data may also be handled by Android's optional backup system or by the storage location you choose when exporting files.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the LexiconTriTrainer Android application and its project website at vekaracodes.github.io/lexicontritrainer/. The app is developed and published under the name VekaraCodes.

It explains what information the app handles on your device, what may leave your device through actions you choose, and what happens when you visit the website.

No accountSign-up is not needed.
Local learning dataPractice history and wordlists stay in app storage.
No app trackingNo ads or app analytics are part of the current design.

2. Information stored on your device

To make the app work, LexiconTriTrainer stores and processes the following information locally:

  • Vocabulary files: built-in example lists and text wordlists you choose to import, stored in the app's private file area.
  • Learning progress: spaced-repetition timestamps and review state associated with vocabulary entries.
  • Preferences: settings such as the selected practice mode, question preferences, and spaced-repetition options.
  • Practice information: answers and session statistics used by the app while you study.

These are used to display exercises, keep track of progress, and remember your settings. The application does not require your name, email address, or an account to study.

If you place personal information in a wordlist yourself, the app will process that content locally as part of the vocabulary file.

3. Data transfers and sharing

The app's normal learning functions do not require an internet connection. The app is not designed to upload vocabulary files, practice answers, or learning progress to VekaraCodes servers. The current app design does not include advertising, analytics, or an account-based synchronization service.

Files you import or export

When you import a wordlist, Android's document picker lets you select a file. The app copies its contents into local app storage. When you export a wordlist, Android lets you choose a destination. If you select a cloud-backed document provider, that provider may store a copy under its own privacy practices. These transfers happen because you choose to import or export a file.

VekaraCodes does not sell or share your wordlists or progress with advertisers.

4. Backups, retention, and deletion

Your imported wordlists and app preferences remain on the device while the app data is kept. Learning progress can be reset, and individual imported wordlists can be deleted using the app's file controls. Uninstalling the app ordinarily removes its private local storage.

Android backups are separate: depending on your Android version, device settings, and the app's backup configuration, Android or your device manufacturer may back up app files and preferences to a backup service. Such backups are controlled by the operating system and the relevant provider, not by VekaraCodes. Data restored from a backup may reappear after reinstalling the app.

To remove local data, use the app's delete/reset controls or uninstall it. To manage or delete cloud backups, use your device's backup settings and the relevant provider's tools. Files you separately exported must also be deleted from the location where you saved them.

5. Visiting this website

This is a static website hosted by GitHub Pages. The website does not require an account, set first-party tracking cookies, load third-party advertising scripts, or run first-party analytics. It uses locally served CSS and a small script that reads the wordlist catalogue from the same site.

GitHub may process connection information such as IP address, browser information, and requests to the pages for operation and security of its hosting service. This is separate from using the Android app. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Following an external link, such as a link to GitHub, takes you to a service with its own privacy practices. Downloading a wordlist from this website also involves a normal web request handled by the host; the downloaded wordlist is not automatically sent from your phone back to the site.

6. Contact, questions, and privacy requests

If you contact VekaraCodes voluntarily, the contact information and message you provide may be used to answer your inquiry or address a support request. It is kept only as long as reasonably needed for that purpose, subject to the email or platform provider's retention and legal requirements. We do not use support correspondence for advertising.

For questions about this policy, contact the developer using the contact options on the VekaraCodes GitHub profile. Please use a private contact channel for personal information; do not post sensitive information in public GitHub issues.

Where applicable under data-protection law, you may ask about, access, correct, or request deletion of personal information that VekaraCodes actually holds, such as information you voluntarily send in a support message. VekaraCodes does not hold a cloud copy of your in-app vocabulary or progress and therefore cannot retrieve or erase that local information remotely.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app, website, or applicable requirements change. The date at the top will be revised to show when the policy was last updated. If the app adds internet features, analytics, accounts, or other data handling in the future, the policy and the relevant store disclosures should be updated accordingly.
